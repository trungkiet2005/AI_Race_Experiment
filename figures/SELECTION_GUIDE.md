# Publication figure selection guide

The manuscript uses the fourteen canonical figure slots listed below. Figures
1, 2, 4, and 8 are author-supplied artwork and are protected from automated
writers. The remaining slots are generated by
`scripts/build_publication_figures.py` from checked-in source tables or
admitted raw runs. Keep the figure order aligned with the paper's story:
mechanism, treatments, theory boundary, aggregate level, diversity, raw-space
geometry, distributions, and the N-player pilot.

| Main figure | Canonical asset | What a reader should learn | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `paper/AIRaceOverview.pdf` | The repeated race, two-player payoff, and N-player payoff are related but distinct objects. | Author-supplied mechanism schematic, not an empirical result. |
| 2 | `paper/ExpOverview.pdf` | Baseline, risk-aware, and social framing are prompt conditions. | Author-supplied prompt schematic; a prompt condition is not a measured model trait. |
| 3 | `paper/egt_theory_vs_llm_unsafe.pdf` | Prompted frontier self-play does not reproduce the reconstructed EGT risk transition. | Descriptive juxtaposition of different behavioural units. |
| 4 | `paper/llm_human_clustering/05b_unsafe_rate_by_group.png` | Aggregate Unsafe rates differ sharply across the tested populations. | Author-supplied artwork; player-race trajectory bootstrap intervals; historical descriptive pilot. |
| 5 | `paper/llm_human_clustering/02_archetype_signatures_and_coverage.pdf` | Similar aggregate levels can hide different coverage of human behavioural diversity. | Human-only exploratory reference space; nearest-centroid resemblance is not mechanism recovery. |
| 6 | `paper/llm_human_clustering/01_tsne_hero_human_left.pdf` | Human trajectories occupy broad raw-sequence geometry, while checkpoints occupy narrower regions. | t-SNE is exploratory and depends on the frozen feature/projection protocol. |
| 7 | `paper/human_vs_llm_distribution.pdf` | Model-specific bands can differ even when a mean is close to the human mean. | Smoothed descriptive distributions; samples and protocols are not exchangeable. |
| 8 | `paper/11_relative_position_grouped_bars.png` | Position associations vary by checkpoint and assigned persona, with non-estimable cells visible. | Author-supplied artwork; N-player pilot; coefficients are observational and not causal rank effects. |

## Promotion rule

Candidate plots in `gallery/` and diagnostic plots in `diagnostics/` remain
available for paper selection, but they are not promoted into the manuscript
without a source path, a run or table hash, an evidence classification, and a
caption that states the estimand. Prefer one clear figure per claim over a
larger gallery of correlated views.
