# Pilot runs: provenance, not evidence

Runs here predate the frozen protocols and are retained so the record of what
was tried is complete. Nothing in the manuscript or the supplement is computed
from them, and no analyser reads this directory.

`ai-race-baseline-20260801/` is a nine-race, three-repetition pilot of
`google/gemini-3-flash-preview` collected on 2026-08-01, recovered from the
Kaggle archive on 2026-09-10. Its manifest carries no `protocol_id` field at
all, which is precisely why it cannot be pooled with, or compared against, the
confirmatory runs: the protocol it ran under was not yet fixed.
